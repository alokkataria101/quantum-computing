Book Knowledge Service

github.com/alokkataria101/quantum-computing / book-knowledge-service/

A production-grade FastAPI microservice that transforms physics PDF textbooks into an intelligent, searchable knowledge base — built with DevOps and AI best practices throughout.

  • Ingests physics textbooks as PDFs; extracts text, detects chapters, and builds a full-text search index (SQLite FTS5) — auto-loading new books dropped into a watch folder at startup
  • Pattern-based question extractor parses Examples, Exercises, Objective I/II, Short Answer sections with correct cross-page ordering and per-question page tracking
  • Web UI (FastAPI + Jinja2) with a page-by-page reader, question browser with type filters, page thumbnails for figure-referencing questions, and AI-powered RAG Q&A
  • IBM watsonx (llama-3-3-70b) integration for chapter-grounded Q&A and AI question generation (MCQ, numerical, short answer) with dynamic token budgeting and graceful degradation
  • Containerised with Docker + docker-compose; shared secrets architecture; Jinja2 auto-reload for zero-restart template iteration
CV Blurb

Deployed via Docker with a shared secrets architecture and zero-downtime template updates.

Quantum Portfolio Intelligence Platform (QPIP)

github.com/alokkataria101/quantum-computing / stock-analyzer/

A 9-page Streamlit investment research dashboard combining quantum-inspired signal processing with IBM watsonx AI for NSE portfolio analysis.

  • Quantum-inspired model uses quantum state similarity matching across historical market states to generate BUY / ADD / HOLD / REDUCE / AVOID signals with probability distributions
  • IBM watsonx (Granite / llama) generates natural-language explanations of each quantum signal — "why BUY?" — and writes full analyst-style investment research reports
  • Portfolio Doctor, What-if Simulator, Opportunity Scanner, intraday live feed, and self-evaluation engine with adaptive memory
  • Containerised with Docker; graceful AI fallback to rule-based explanations when credentials absent
CV Blurb

Features live intraday feeds, adaptive memory, portfolio diagnostics, and a containerised DevOps deployment.

Quantum Computing & Physics Research

github.com/alokkataria101/quantum-computing / (Hamiltonian, VQE, QFT, phase-estimation)

End-to-end quantum computing experiments from first principles — Schrödinger equation solvers through to Qiskit circuit implementations on real quantum hardware simulators.

  • Solved the time-independent Schrödinger equation numerically (finite differences) for particle-in-a-box and hydrogen atom; implemented Hartree–Fock and compared against VQE
  • Five-stage VQE tutorial: Hartree–Fock → helium → H₂ dissociation curve → CO₂ active space → CCSD benchmark; validates quantum chemistry on Qiskit Aer simulators
  • Quantum Fourier Transform applied to financial time series (NSE stocks via Yahoo Finance) and arbitrary mathematical functions — comparing QFT vs classical FFT frequency extraction
  • Full Quantum Phase Estimation implementation from single-qubit measurement to multi-qubit QPE; Grover's and Shor's algorithm demonstrations
CV Blurb
Conducting independent research in quantum computing and quantum chemistry — implemented VQE for molecular ground-state energy estimation, Quantum Phase Estimation, Grover/Shor algorithms, and Quantum Fourier Transform analysis of financial time series using Qiskit. Bridges theoretical quantum physics (Schrödinger equation, Hartree–Fock) with practical circuit-level Qiskit implementations.
